================================================================================
ECOPULSE AI - SHIRPUR MUNICIPAL COUNCIL
OFFICIAL META WHATSAPP CLOUD API SETUP GUIDE (STEP-BY-STEP)
================================================================================

This guide explains how to connect your EcoPulse AI backend to the real, original
WhatsApp using Meta's official WhatsApp Business Cloud API.

Meta provides a 100% FREE tier including 1,000 service conversations every month.
No credit card or new SIM card is needed to get started; Meta provides a free test
phone number immediately.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PREREQUISITES:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. A standard personal Facebook account.
2. An internet browser on your computer.
3. Your personal smartphone with WhatsApp installed.

================================================================================
STEP 1: LOG IN TO META DEVELOPER PORTAL
================================================================================
1. Open your browser and go to:
   https://developers.facebook.com/

2. Click 'Log In' in the top-right corner using your existing Facebook account.

3. If this is your first time:
   - Click 'Get Started'.
   - Accept the Meta Developer Terms.
   - Select your role (e.g., 'Developer' or 'Other') and verify your account.

================================================================================
STEP 2: CREATE A NEW META APP
================================================================================
1. In the top-right navigation bar, click 'My Apps'.
2. Click the green/blue button labeled 'Create App'.
3. On the screen 'What do you want your app to do?':
   - Select 'Other' -> Click 'Next'.
4. On the screen 'Select an app type':
   - Select 'Business' -> Click 'Next'.
5. Enter App Details:
   - App Name: EcoPulse Shirpur (or any name you choose)
   - App Contact Email: Your email address
   - Business Account: You can leave this as 'None' or select your business
6. Click 'Create App' (enter your Facebook password if prompted).

================================================================================
STEP 3: ADD WHATSAPP TO YOUR APP
================================================================================
1. You will be taken to the 'Add products to your app' dashboard.
2. Scroll down until you find the 'WhatsApp' card.
3. Click the 'Set up' button on the WhatsApp card.
4. Click 'Continue' to accept Meta's WhatsApp terms.

================================================================================
STEP 4: GET YOUR SECRET API KEYS
================================================================================
You are now on the 'API Setup' screen.
Look at the center of the page to find your 2 key credentials:

1. TEMPORARY ACCESS TOKEN:
   - A long string starting with 'EAAG...'
   - Click the 'Copy' button next to it.
   (Note: This temporary token lasts for 24 hours. For permanent production tokens,
   see the 'Permanent Token Setup' section below).

2. PHONE NUMBER ID:
   - A numerical string (e.g., 104829104829104).
   - Copy this number.

3. OPEN YOUR PROJECT FILE:
   Navigate to 'server/.env' in your code editor and paste these values:

   WHATSAPP_PROVIDER=meta
   WHATSAPP_TOKEN="EAAG...paste_your_token_here..."
   WHATSAPP_PHONE_NUMBER_ID="104829104829104"
   WHATSAPP_VERIFY_TOKEN="ecopulse_whatsapp_token_2026"
   WHATSAPP_BUSINESS_PHONE="+919876543210"

================================================================================
STEP 5: WHITELIST YOUR TEST PHONE NUMBER
================================================================================
Before sending messages with a test number, Meta requires you to verify the
recipient's personal WhatsApp number:

1. On the same 'API Setup' page, find 'Step 1: Select phone numbers'.
2. In the 'To' dropdown menu, click 'Manage phone number list'.
3. Select Country: 'India (+91)' and enter your personal WhatsApp mobile number.
4. Click 'Next'. Meta will send a 6-digit verification code to your WhatsApp.
5. Enter the 6-digit code on the Meta screen and click 'Verify'.
   (Your personal phone is now authorized to receive test messages).

================================================================================
STEP 6: CONFIGURE THE WEBHOOK (LINKING WHATSAPP TO YOUR BACKEND)
================================================================================
The webhook allows Meta to forward citizen messages, photos, and locations
to your FastAPI backend.

1. In the left-hand sidebar menu, expand 'WhatsApp' and click 'Configuration'.
2. In the 'Webhook' section, click the 'Edit' button.
3. Enter the following details:

   CALLBACK URL:
   - For Production (Render):
     https://<your-render-backend-url>/api/whatsapp/webhook
     (Example: https://city-swap-backend.onrender.com/api/whatsapp/webhook)

   - For Local Testing on your Computer:
     Use ngrok (run: ngrok http 8000) and enter:
     https://<your-ngrok-subdomain>.ngrok-free.app/api/whatsapp/webhook

   VERIFY TOKEN:
     ecopulse_whatsapp_token_2026
     (Must match WHATSAPP_VERIFY_TOKEN in server/.env)

4. Click 'Verify and save'.
   Meta will send a test GET handshake request to your server.
   Your backend will verify the token and return a success checkmark.

5. Under 'Webhook fields', click the 'Manage' button.
6. Look for the row named 'messages' and click 'Subscribe'.
7. Click 'Done'.

================================================================================
STEP 7: TEST ON ORIGINAL WHATSAPP!
================================================================================
1. Go back to 'WhatsApp' -> 'API Setup' in the left menu.
2. Check the 'From' field: Meta displays its test phone number (e.g., +1 555...).
3. Save this number in your phone's contacts as 'EcoPulse Test' or click
   the link on the screen to open the chat directly in WhatsApp.
4. Send this message from your personal WhatsApp:

   JOIN SHP-W12-H1042

5. You will instantly receive the live bilingual EcoPulse citizen dashboard:
   - Reply '1' to report waste (send photo & location).
   - Reply '2' to track complaints.
   - Reply '3' to check your EcoCoin balance & municipal tax rebate value.
   - Reply '4' to view the live location of the garbage collection truck!
   - Reply '6' to switch language between Marathi and English.

================================================================================
HOW TO GET A PERMANENT (NEVER-EXPIRING) ACCESS TOKEN FOR PRODUCTION
================================================================================
Temporary tokens expire after 24 hours. For 24/7 municipal deployment:

1. Open Meta Business Manager Settings:
   https://business.facebook.com/settings/
2. In the left menu, click 'Users' -> 'System users'.
3. Click 'Add' -> Name it 'EcoPulse-Bot' -> Select Role: 'Admin'.
4. Click 'Generate new token' -> Select your App ('EcoPulse Shirpur').
5. Under Token Expiration, select 'Never'.
6. Check these 2 permissions:
   - whatsapp_business_messaging
   - whatsapp_business_management
7. Click 'Generate Token' and copy this permanent token into your 'server/.env':
   WHATSAPP_TOKEN="EAAG..."

================================================================================
HOW TO GENERATE HOUSEHOLD QR CODE STICKERS FOR PRINTING
================================================================================
To generate QR code stickers for all 27 wards in Shirpur, call the backend API:

ENDPOINT:
POST /api/whatsapp/generate-qr
Content-Type: application/json

PAYLOAD:
{
  "ward": 12,
  "household_number": 1042
}

RESPONSE:
{
  "household_code": "SHP-W12-H1042",
  "ward": "Ward 12",
  "household_number": 1042,
  "qr_scan_text": "JOIN SHP-W12-H1042",
  "whatsapp_direct_link": "https://wa.me/919876543210?text=JOIN%20SHP-W12-H1042"
}

Use any free QR code generator (or Python qrcode library) to convert the
'whatsapp_direct_link' into a scannable sticker for municipal dustbins and gates!

================================================================================
TROUBLESHOOTING & COMMON MISTAKES
================================================================================
1. ERROR: 'Verification token mismatch' when saving Webhook:
   -> Make sure WHATSAPP_VERIFY_TOKEN in server/.env is exactly:
      ecopulse_whatsapp_token_2026

2. Messages sent from WhatsApp do not trigger a reply:
   -> Ensure you clicked 'Subscribe' on the 'messages' row in Webhook fields (Step 6).
   -> Ensure your personal phone number is added to the 'To' list (Step 5).

3. Error 401 / Invalid OAuth Token:
   -> The 24-hour temporary token has expired. Copy a new token from the API Setup
      page or generate a permanent System User token (see above).
